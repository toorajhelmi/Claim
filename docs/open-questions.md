# Open Questions

## Payments and legal

- Which payment/escrow partner will support this model?
- Should supporters pay immediately or authorize payment until success?
- Which states/countries should be excluded initially?
- Is Claim acting as escrow, marketplace, fundraiser, creator monetization platform, or something else?
- What KYC/AML checks are required for claimants and supporters?
- What tax reporting is required for claimant payouts, charity donations, and platform fees?
- Can Claim custody stake funds directly, or must a regulated partner hold funds?
- Can creators earn supporter pledge pools without triggering money-transmission, fundraising, contest, or other regulated activity concerns?

## Failure routing

- Which charities are supported at launch?
- Who controls and verifies failed-stake charity payouts?
- Should supporter rebate ever be allowed, and in which jurisdictions?
- If supporter pledge donation is optional, when does the supporter choose refund vs donation?
- Are platform fees taken only from successful supporter payouts?

## Supporter incentives

- Are supporter NFTs free proof collectibles, paid add-ons, or minted only on success?
- Are supporter badges enough without financial upside?
- Which supporter perks convert best for challenge/proof audiences?
- Should supporters get private updates during the claim or only final proof?
- What language makes supporter pledging feel like backing a creator event, not betting on an outcome?

## Verification and disputes

- How should disputes be handled if livestream, video, third-party record, referee note, or checklist evidence is ambiguous?
- Who is the human arbitration authority?
- What is the evidence submission deadline?
- What is the appeal window?
- What happens if TikTok, YouTube, Twitch, Kick, Stripe, Loom, GitHub, Vercel, or another third-party service is unavailable during review?
- How should prior work, training, preparation, coaching, editing, and off-camera assistance be disclosed?
- Which proof events must be generated automatically by Claim versus manually added by recorder/reviewer?
- How should Claim handle dropped live streams, reconnects, or missing segments during a mobile claim?
- What evidence is enough for public statement/confrontation claims: video, audio, transcript, witness note, or all of them?

## Live rooms and recorders

- Should MVP live rooms use LiveKit, another provider, or embedded external streams?
- How many live video feeds should MVP support: challenger only, challenger plus one recorder, or more?
- Can recorders receive a fixed share of the payout pool, and when is that split locked?
- What permissions should recorder/witness roles have during the live claim?
- Should supporters interact through free chat, structured votes, directions, prompts, or all of them?
- How should live chat directions be selected for city-walk claims: challenger choice, supporter vote, moderator selection, or automatic winner?
- Should Claim store full recordings, short clips around proof events, or both?

## Product and launch

- Should claims be public by default or invite-only for early pilots?
- What is the minimum claimant stake that signals seriousness without blocking adoption?
- What maximum stake/pledge caps are safest for the closed pilot?
- Should Claim start as concierge pages before account creation exists?
- Should initial claim pages be editable only by Claim operators?
- What is the first cohort date and selection process?
- Which live mission categories create the strongest pledge intent: city walk, public statement/confrontation, supporter-directed mission, endurance, or high-tension event?
- What evidence is enough to move from landing page plus concierge validation into building full auth, payments, and dashboards?
- What pledge threshold mechanics best motivate challengers to share the preview page before the live event?
