# Current Direction

## Public positioning

Claim is a platform for making claims, backing them, and proving whether they happened.

Short positioning options:

- Say it. Stake it. Prove it.
- Make a claim. Back the attempt. Verify the outcome.
- The proof layer for internet claims.

Avoid leading with narrow category language such as "AI builder app," "positive challenge," or "crowdfunding for apps." Those may be useful subcategories later, but the main product should stay broad.

## First wedge

The strongest initial wedge is live, audience-backed claims with visible tension and proof.

These are claims that:

- are easy to understand quickly;
- have a visible attempt or journey;
- are livestreamable;
- have a clear proof condition;
- can create audience suspense;
- give the challenger a meaningful monetary incentive.

The MVP should be stress-tested against two aggressive formats:

1. **Live chat city walk** — the challenger crosses a city by a deadline using only live chat directions, with timestamped check-ins on stream.
2. **Public confrontation / public statement moment** — the challenger asks or says a pre-declared thing during a public moment, with live recording and timestamped proof.

These are technical stress tests for claim creation, social sharing, pledges, recorder/witness roles, live streams, supporter interaction, evidence, and verification. They do not mean every submitted claim in these categories should automatically be approved.

## Challenger incentive

The challenger should have clear monetary upside:

1. They make a claim.
2. Supporters pledge to back/watch the attempt.
3. If the challenger proves the claim, they earn the supporter pledge pool minus platform fee.
4. If the challenger fails, supporter pledges are refunded or donated according to locked claim rules, and the challenger's stake routes according to the locked failure option.

This makes Claim a paid public event format for creators, not just a verification utility.

## Supporter incentive

Supporters should not be promised financial upside by default.

Supporters back claims for:

- access;
- updates;
- entertainment;
- status;
- supporter wall credit;
- community participation;
- perks from the creator;
- the satisfaction of helping the attempt happen.

Supporter rebate or financial upside is a separate high-risk model that requires legal and payment-provider review.

## Validation plan

Build toward a web MVP that proves the complete live-claim loop.

Start with:

1. Landing page.
2. Example claim cards.
3. Creator application form.
4. Supporter waitlist or pledge-interest form.
5. Concierge claim pages.
6. Claim preview pages for sharing to social and direct connections.
7. Manual pledge/precommit flow.
8. Recorder/witness invitation flow.
9. Live room prototype.
10. Manual evidence review.
11. Final proof/result page.
