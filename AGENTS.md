# Claim Agent Instructions

Claim is a standalone product and repository. It is unrelated to CeenAiX.

## Product constraints

- Claim is a broad platform for public claims and evidence-backed verification.
- Public positioning should be simple: make a claim, stake it, let supporters back/watch it, and prove it.
- Initial validation should focus on live, audience-backed claims that are time-bound, high-tension, livestreamable, and objectively verifiable.
- MVP product decisions should be stress-tested against `docs/mvp-live-proof-flows.md`, especially live chat city walk and public confrontation/public statement moments.
- Do not frame Claim as a betting market.
- Do not support yes/no positions, odds, trading, or variable betting-style payouts.
- Challenger monetary upside should come from earning a supporter pledge pool after proving the claim.
- Do not promise supporter financial upside by default.
- Treat supporter rebate, NFTs, escrow, charity routing, taxes, and KYC/AML as legal/payment-provider review items before implementation.
- Keep MVP claim categories narrow and template-driven.
- AI may summarize and prepare evidence, but humans must decide disputed outcomes.
- Keep trust-and-safety rules as operating constraints; public copy does not need to lead with "positive" or "safe" positioning.
- Claim preview pages, social sharing, pledge thresholds, recorder/witness roles, live rooms, timestamped proof events, and result pages are core MVP primitives.

## Initial build posture

Prioritize validation and concierge operations before automated payments:

1. Landing page and segmented waitlist.
2. Claim preview and social sharing flow.
3. Creator outreach with the paid-live-mission pitch.
4. Concierge claim setup.
5. Recorder/witness invitation support.
6. Strict templates and moderation before public listing.
7. Manual verification and dispute review.
